Why APEXGRIND? (Track 3 Solution)
High Noise: Smart BLDC speed matching + noise band indicator replaces constant high-RPM grinding.
Overheating: Dual thermal guard + predictive slope protection pauses before food proteins break down.
Hard to Clean: Fixed 500mL small jar + easy-detach modular attachments with hygiene usage counters.
Service Issues: Automated wear trends and coupler hour tracking prevent sudden breakdowns.
Texture Feedback: Load-cell mass sensing + digital texture modes (Coarse, Medium, Fine, Paste).
Capacity Constraints: Dual jar setup (500mL compact jar + 1.5L multi-task jar with 6 attachments).
